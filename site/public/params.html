Archive

Every Filecoin node needs the Groth16 proving and verifying keys listed in parameters.json, about 345 GB in 44 files, normally fetched from proofs.filecoin.io. ChainVault fetches each file, checks the manifest digest, and stores it on a Filecoin PDP storage provider in 1000 MiB pieces with a manifest keyed by the file's original CID.

Use

Point a node here instead of the default gateway. It fetches by CID as usual and verifies the digest itself, so a copy is byte-exact or rejected.
